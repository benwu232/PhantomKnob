Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

At Phantom Knob, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our application (the "Software") and website.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Input Device Data: The Software monitors touch coordinates on your multi-touch trackpad and mouse gestures locally to analyze and convert them into virtual scroll and key rotation actions. This input data is processed strictly in real-time memory and is never recorded, stored locally, or sent to external servers.

1.2 Custom Configuration Rules: The Software allows you to create customized rules for controlling specific applications. If you enable iCloud sync, these rules are stored in your personal iCloud container database managed by Apple. We do not have access to your iCloud sync data.

1.3 License and Registration Information: When you purchase or activate a Pro license, our third-party merchant of record, Paddle, collects payment and license details. The Software stores your registration key locally in your macOS Keychain to verify your license status.

2. Third-Party Services

We use Paddle as our third-party billing merchant. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. When the Software validates your license key, it communicates directly with Paddle's activation servers to authenticate your purchase.

3. Security

We implement security best practices (such as utilizing the secure macOS Keychain for license storage) to protect your local configurations. No personal or telemetry data is harvested from your system.

4. Compliance with Accessibility APIs

Phantom Knob requests macOS Accessibility permission to perform window focus detection, identify slider elements under the cursor, and inject input scroll events. We do not use these permissions to monitor, collect, or upload keystrokes or screen captures.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post any adjustments on this page and update the "Last Updated" timestamp.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@phantomknob.com.

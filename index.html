04 / ADVANCED

Deep Dive · Under the Hood & Advanced Customization

For tech enthusiasts and pro power users. PhantomKnob works seamlessly out of the box without needing to know any of this.

▸ 01 / How Knob Gestures Work Under the Hood +

1 · Identify Target Controls

When the mouse hovers over the screen, the system uses macOS Accessibility APIs to identify the hovered window and active slider elements within milliseconds.

2 · Detect & Trap Knob Gestures

Determines whether current trackpad contact represents an intentional twirl gesture. If so, raw multitouch events are trapped to avoid conflict with native swipes.

3 · Geometric Calculation

Extracts coordinate pairs from the primary touchpoints, reconstructs the virtual knob geometry, and calculates continuous angular deltas mapped to variable values.

4 · Event Synthesis & Direct Injection

Translates rotational deltas into arrow keys, scroll events, or AX commands, injecting them into the macOS global event tap stream for instantaneous response.

5 · GPU-Accelerated Rendering

Utilizes Metal and CoreAnimation for hardware-accelerated HUD motion, delivering silky visual feedback with negligible CPU footprint.

6 · Single-Finger Flywheel (Pro)

When lifting one finger, Pro locks the virtual center with 180° phase offset compensation for endless single-finger spinning; Free applies a smooth physical damping stop.

▸ 02 / How to Customize Knobs for Any Application +

1 · Identify Target Input Method

Knob gestures translate rotation into input modes acceptable by target UI elements—such as vertical scroll, horizontal scroll, arrow keys, or dragging. A quick experiment reveals the right mode.

2 · Configure in Knob Manager

Hover over the target slider and perform a knob gesture. Press the C key to open the Knob Manager, select the identified behavior, and save your custom rule instantly.

3 · Visual Styling & Sensitivities

Customize knob naming, knob styles (Single, Double-ring, CVK), accent colors, rotation sensitivity multipliers, and center icon stamp designs.
